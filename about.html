Our Story

North Star Bakery is a neighborhood bakery built around a simple idea: fresh food brings people together. We begin baking early so our community can start the day with bread and pastries made with care.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We value simple ingredients, local grains when available, and thoughtful preparation. We believe good baking takes time, so we focus on quality rather than rushing the process.

Our Team

Bakers

Our bakers prepare breads, pastries, and seasonal specialties in small batches throughout the week.

Front Counter Team

Our front counter team welcomes customers, answers product questions, and helps coordinate pre-orders for families and events.

Our Community

We are proud to serve local customers, commuters, families, and event planners. Whether you are stopping in for breakfast or planning a celebration, we want every visit to feel warm and welcoming.
